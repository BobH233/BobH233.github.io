Hi, it’s me!

I'm a second-year master's student in Control Science and Engineering at the School of Automation, Beijing Institute of Technology, and a member of the Integrated Navigation and Intelligent Navigation (ININ) Laboratory.

My previous research focused on 3D Gaussian Splatting SLAM, scene reconstruction, and lifelong scene maintenance in dynamic environments.

I'm currently learning about Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models and generative methods, especially Flow Matching. My goal is to build robotic technology that people can use and benefit from in everyday life.
